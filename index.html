01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Quality is not a phase.
It’s a way of thinking.

I work at the intersection of product intent and real-world experience—making sure the things we build do what people need them to do.

As a Software Quality Assurance Engineer at Priyoshop.com Ltd., I analyze requirements, shape test plans, manage defects, and collaborate across teams. From functional and regression testing to end-to-end business workflows, I bring a detail-oriented, user-first perspective to every release.

I believe great QA starts with asking better questions, documenting clearly, and working as one team throughout the software development lifecycle.
